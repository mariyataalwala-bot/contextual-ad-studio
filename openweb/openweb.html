How it works
  1. Inventory — the news and lifestyle sites we read, and the ad formats each sells. Click latest articles to see a site's newest stories.
  2. Campaign — add the brand, dates, a short brief (what it sells and when people need it — articles are matched by meaning, not only keywords) and the creative file (put it in the creatives folder first). Without an active campaign nothing is recommended.
  3. Fetch & analyse — reads the newest articles, checks they're safe for the brand, and matches them to your campaigns. Click an article to see why it matched.
  4. Make mock — shows your creative on the real article in a phone-sized screenshot, watermarked MOCK. Nothing goes live.
